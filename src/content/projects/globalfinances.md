## Context

GlobalFinances is accounting and electronic invoicing software for an Ecuador-based firm. I worked as a Full-Stack Developer and Tech Lead.

## My contribution

I contributed to accounting software and an invoicing module while leading a team of three developers. My work combined hands-on engineering with technical coordination.

## Technical scope

The project stack includes Angular, Laravel, and PostgreSQL. The frontend supported financial workflows, with backend services handling the corresponding application operations.

## Platform scale

The platform processed approximately **80,000 invoices per month**. This is a platform-level measure of the system's workload, not a claim that I individually produced that result.

## Leadership context

Leading three developers was part of the role alongside implementation work. That combination is relevant to my engineering experience: contributing directly while coordinating technical delivery within a small team.
