## Context

Brikmate provides document interaction and PDF reading experiences for lease information. I worked on the project through Jobsity as a Full-Stack Developer.

## My contribution

I built PDF reading and document chat interactions that linked answers back to the relevant lease sections. The work connected a conversational interface with the document material a user needed to inspect.

## Engineering scope

The stack includes Next.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL. My documented contribution concerns the reading and interaction experience; it does not imply ownership of the underlying AI models or model infrastructure.

## Source navigation

Linking an answer to the relevant document section gives users a way to inspect its context. This was a concrete part of the interface work, rather than a claim about model accuracy or document understanding quality.
