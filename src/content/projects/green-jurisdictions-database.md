## Context

Green Jurisdictions Database is an environmental data platform from Earth Innovation Institute. The public experience supports discovering jurisdictions and exploring environmental information. Tropical Fire Observatory is a second surface of the same project, with its own domain and a map-based interface.

## My contribution

As a Full-Stack Developer, I worked on interactive maps, search, and data exports. My responsibilities included frontend features, Laravel APIs, Docker-based development, and GitHub Actions workflows.

My contribution was part of a wider team effort. The public screenshots illustrate the product context, rather than individual ownership of the full interface or dataset.

## Engineering scope

The stack spans Next.js and React on the frontend, Laravel APIs, and PostgreSQL. The work crossed the boundary between finding information in an interface and making it available through backend services and exports.

Docker-based development and GitHub Actions were also part of my responsibilities.
